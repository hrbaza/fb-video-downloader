FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I download private videos?

No. Only public videos work. Videos from private profiles or closed groups need a login, so they can't be fetched.

Where does the video get saved?

In your browser's usual Downloads folder. On a phone, look in the Files or Downloads app.

Why don't I see an HD option?

Some videos were only uploaded in standard quality, so Facebook only offers SD for them.

Does it work with reels?

Yes. Reels, Watch videos, page videos and fb.watch short links all work.
